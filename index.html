Progression

  • Train most working sets with 1-2 reps in reserve.
  • Add reps before adding weight.
  • When all sets hit the top of the rep range with clean form, add load next time.
  • If performance drops for two sessions, hold load and reduce one set per exercise for a week.

Nutrition

  • Average 2150-2250 calories per day.
  • Protein target: 165-190g per day.
  • Keep breakfast low-carb; place carbs before and after the 5pm lift.
  • Aim for 2 cups fruit and 3 cups vegetables daily.
